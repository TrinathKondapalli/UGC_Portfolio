Meet Aanya, our AI creator

Aanya is a fictional, AI-generated creator. She keeps the same face and voice across every ad, so your brand gets a consistent presenter without casting or shoot days.

  • Every ad is labelled AI-generated.
  • Aanya never claims she bought, owns or used a product.
  • Product benefits are presented as the brand's own claims.
  • No fake reviews, no invented results.

Questions

What do I get?

A finished vertical ad (9:16) for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, plus alternative opening hooks so you can test which one stops the scroll.

Is the free sample really free?

Yes. We take a limited number of free samples each week so we can give each one proper attention. Send your product on WhatsApp to request one.

Will people know it's AI?

Yes. Every ad carries an AI-generated label and disclosure. We believe that's the only way to use an AI creator responsibly.

Can you use a different face or language?

English with Aanya is available now. More AI creators and Indian languages are planned. Ask us about your needs.

Which products work best?

Products with a clear visual moment: a shade, a texture, sparkle, fit, or a function you can show in a few seconds.
